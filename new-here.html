New Here

Welcome — we’re glad you’re here

Thinking about visiting Our Savior? Here’s everything you need to feel at home before you even walk through the door.

Your First Visit

What to expect, step by step

1

Arrive & be welcomed

Park out front and come in the main entrance. There’s no dress code — come as you are — and a friendly face will help you find your way.

2

Worship together

Our service centers on the forgiveness of Jesus, proclaimed in Scripture, hymns, and the Sacraments. Everything you need to follow along is provided.

3

Stay & connect

Join us for Bible class and fellowship afterward. We’d love to answer your questions and get to know you.
