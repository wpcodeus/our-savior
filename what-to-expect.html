What to Expect

Your worship experience

Since the forgiveness of Jesus is the central message of the Bible, it is also the central focus of our worship services.

Worship at Our Savior

Our worship services blend ancient and contemporary elements. Lutheran worship has two key aspects that shape everything we do together.

First

The Gospel predominates

The good news of forgiveness is proclaimed in Scripture readings, sermons, and hymns, and carried out in the celebration of the Sacraments:

  • Holy Baptism — God washes away sin, creates faith, and adopts us as his children.
  • Holy Communion — Jesus gives his true body and blood for the forgiveness of sins and the strengthening of faith.
Second

Worship is participatory

How can the people internalize the message of forgiveness if they are simply passive observers? Our songs and liturgy are designed to be sung by everyone, regardless of talent level.

The creeds and parts of the prayers are spoken by everyone, because God wants to hear your voice just as much as the pastor’s. The truth of forgiveness found in the liturgy becomes yours when it is spoken by your mouth.
