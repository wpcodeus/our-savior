What We Believe

Rooted in God’s Word

Our Savior in Kasson, MN is a confessional Lutheran church that believes the Bible is the inspired and error-free Word of God.

“For God so loved the world, that he gave his one and only Son, that whoever believes in him shall not perish but have eternal life.”John 3:16

Law & Gospel

Rooted in God’s Word

We believe the Bible contains both Law and Gospel: the Law reveals our inability, due to our sinful nature, to reach God or Heaven on our own, while the Gospel shares that God, through his love and mercy, reached out to us. This was accomplished by Jesus Christ, who met all the requirements needed for us to enter Heaven.

Our Confession

Confessing the faith

We joyfully embrace the teachings of the Wisconsin Evangelical Lutheran Synod (WELS). These confessions are a faithful summary of the Bible’s teachings and help us remain united in doctrine and purpose, shaping the way we live and serve together as a congregation and synod.

Read the WELS Confessions
Living as Christ’s Church

How we live it out

We believe that all believers form the one holy Christian Church. We uphold God’s design for life, marriage, and family, recognizing every person as created and loved by God. In all things, we seek to glorify God and share the hope we have in Christ, joining together to proclaim the gospel, nurture believers, and serve our community with Christ’s love.

In Summary

What we believe

  • We believe that the entire Bible is God’s inspired, inerrant, unchanging revelation to mankind.
  • We believe that God reveals himself as three distinct yet equal persons in one divine Being: Father, Son, and Holy Spirit.
  • We believe that all people have inherited a sinful nature from Adam and are by that nature spiritually dead, hostile to God, and incapable of believing in him.
  • We believe that God is perfectly just and must punish every sin for eternity, yet he is perfectly loving and wants all people to be saved from their sins.
  • We believe that God promised and sent his Son, Jesus Christ, as a human being to live the perfect life he requires of us and to be punished for sin in our place. Because Jesus is also God, his life and death count for all sin of all people of all time.
  • We believe that Jesus’ saving work becomes ours only by faith. This faith is a gift from God, which the Holy Spirit creates in us through the good news of the gospel.
  • We believe that God wants believers to share the good news with all people, so that “whoever believes in him will not perish but have eternal life.” (John 3:16)
  • We believe that faith lives and grows through the continued hearing of God’s Word and partaking of his Sacraments — baptism and the Lord’s Supper.

Have questions about faith?

Pastor Christie would love to sit down and talk through any questions you have about God’s Word. There’s no pressure — just conversation.
