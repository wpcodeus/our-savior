Welcome to Our Savior · Kasson, MN

Come and know the grace of Jesus.

We’re a WELS Lutheran congregation gathered around one message — the forgiveness of sins won for you in Jesus Christ. Wherever you are on your journey of faith, there’s a place for you here.

Welcome home

A gathering of Christians, centered on the gospel.

Thank you for looking at our website. Whether you’re exploring faith for the first time, new to the area, or looking for a church home, we’d love to meet you.

The continuing mission of Our Savior Evangelical Lutheran Church, as a gathering of Christians, is to serve all people in God’s world with the gospel of Jesus Christ on the basis of the Holy Scriptures.

What a first visit looks like

1

Arrive & be welcomed

Park out front and come in the main entrance. A greeter will help you find your way — and there’s no dress code.

2

Worship together

Our service blends timeless liturgy and hymns with the one message at the center of it all: forgiveness in Jesus. Everything you need is provided.

3

Stay & connect

Join us for Bible class and fellowship afterward. We’d love to answer your questions and get to know you.

What we believe

“For God so loved the world, that he gave his one and only Son, that whoever believes in him shall not perish but have eternal life.”John 3:16

We are a confessional Lutheran church of the Wisconsin Evangelical Lutheran Synod (WELS). We believe the Bible is God’s inspired, inerrant Word — and that salvation is God’s free gift, received by grace through faith in Jesus Christ alone.
