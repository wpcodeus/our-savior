Welcome Home

A gathering of Christians, centered on the gospel.

Thank you for looking at our website. Whether you’re exploring faith for the first time, new to the area, or looking for a church home, we’d love to meet you.

The continuing mission of Our Savior, as a gathering of Christians, is to serve all people in God’s world with the gospel of Jesus Christ on the basis of the Holy Scriptures.

New Here?

What a first visit looks like

Come as you are — there’s no dress code and no pressure. Here’s what to expect when you join us.

1

Arrive & be welcomed

Park out front and come in the main entrance. A friendly face will help you find your way.

2

Worship together

Our service centers on the forgiveness of Jesus — proclaimed in Scripture, hymns, and the Sacraments. Everything you need is provided.

3

Stay & connect

Join us for Bible class and fellowship afterward. We’d love to answer your questions and get to know you.
