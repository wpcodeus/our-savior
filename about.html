About Us

A gathering of Christians in Kasson

Thank you for looking at our website. Learn our story, meet our pastor, and get to know the people who serve Our Savior Lutheran.

Our Mission

The continuing mission of Our Savior Evangelical Lutheran Church, as a gathering of Christians, is to serve all people in God’s world with the gospel of Jesus Christ based on the Holy Scriptures. We believe that the Bible is the final authority in all matters of doctrine, that it is fully inspired by God and without error.

We are a member of the Wisconsin Evangelical Lutheran Synod (WELS), and as such, we are bound together with other churches to support the education of teachers and pastors, support Christian life, and encourage the spread of the Good News of salvation through Jesus Christ.

Our Story

How Our Savior began

The idea of starting our congregation began in 1991, with about ten families in Dodge County with Lutheran backgrounds who met with Pastor Leroy Lothert, a semi-retired minister who served in a prison ministry and visited families at Rochester hospitals. They hosted a public meeting in a Kasson restaurant to determine the interest in establishing a WELS mission in Kasson.

The first worship service led by Pastor Lothert was held on Saturday, January 4, 1992, with 38 souls at Mt. Sion Lutheran Church in Kasson. By June 1992, the group adopted a church constitution and continued to meet on Saturday evenings. On April 4, 1993, the congregation started Sunday morning services in a Kasson-Mantorville Public School lunchroom. Pastor Lothert was installed on August 15, 1993.

Over twelve acres of farmland was purchased in northwest Kasson in September 1995. With the paving of the county road, the location became a desirable area for housing. Five acres were sold to allow the church to be debt free. Builders for Christ came to Kasson to work with our members to build a sanctuary, fellowship hall, office, and library in July of 1998.

As the congregation grew, we decided to add an education addition to our building. This was completed in 2012 and completely paid off in 2024. This addition allows for better Sunday School instruction and provides an area for organizational meetings, Bible studies, and potentially, a preschool.

A Note from the Pastor

Hi, I’m Pastor Christie

I received my Master’s of Divinity from Wisconsin Lutheran Seminary in May of 2023. My wife Rachel and I have been married since 2020 and have been blessed with two children, Charlotte and Hans. My favorite hobbies include Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu, listening to podcasts, and doing just about anything outdoors.

My favorite part of the job is writing and preaching sermons. I also love to help people connect the dots in Scripture and understand the historical context of the Biblical world. I get excited about opportunities to simply sit down and answer questions about God’s Word. If you have any, I’d love to hear them!
