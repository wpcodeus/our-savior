Catechism

The faith, clearly taught

Using Luther’s Small Catechism, our catechism instruction grounds young people in the core truths of Scripture and prepares them to confess their faith.

About Catechism

Six chief parts, one Savior

Catechism instruction walks students through the six chief parts of Christian teaching — the Ten Commandments, the Apostles’ Creed, the Lord’s Prayer, Baptism, Confession, and the Lord’s Supper. Along the way, students come to know their Savior more deeply and are prepared for confirmation and a lifetime in the Word.
