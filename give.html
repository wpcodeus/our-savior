Give

Support the work of the gospel

Your generosity helps us share the good news of Jesus in Kasson and beyond — supporting worship, teaching, and the care of our community.

Ways to Give

Three simple ways to give

Give Online

Make a secure one-time or recurring gift through our giving partner, Vanco. It only takes a minute.

Give Online Now

In Person

Give during Sunday worship using the offering plate or a giving envelope. Envelopes are available at church.

By Mail

Mail your gift to: Our Savior Lutheran Church, 600 8th Ave NW, Kasson, MN 55944.

“Each one must give as he has decided in his heart, not reluctantly or under compulsion, for God loves a cheerful giver.”2 Corinthians 9:7
